01 · Why now

Companies already use AI. What's missing is someone to make sure it works safely.

Employees in sales, finance, and HR are already building apps and agents. Each one connects data, spreadsheets, and customers. Almost no one reviews, measures, or teaches how to do it right. That's where Profissional 2030 comes in.

07 · Questions

Before you start

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The five courses and the lab are open and free, on INEMA.CLUB. The certification, when it opens, will have its own rules.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You will use AI tools to build and review, but the focus is method: diagnosing, assessing risk, following up, and teaching. When code appears, it comes ready to copy, with an explanation of what to look for.

Where do I start?

Start with the common Base (about 2 h 20). Then choose the track closest to your work: Consultant if you talk to owners, Auditor if you come from IT or compliance, Mentor if you coach managers, Trainer if you teach classes or trainings.

How long does it take?

Each course has 8 lessons of 15 to 25 minutes, plus practice. The whole program adds up to about 13 hours of lessons. Your progress is saved in the browser.

Do the lessons become outdated quickly?

The tools change every month; the method doesn't. That's why each course ends with a "What changed" panel, with dated facts and sources, reviewed every quarter.

Is it available in English and Spanish?

For now, only in Portuguese. The English and Spanish versions are coming next.
